Business Insights

\n

With 996 clients and ₹130.49 billion in AUM, the average client holding is substantial, indicating a high-value client base. However, revenue of only ₹2.95 million suggests limited fee generation relative to the size of the asset base. The investment inflow of ₹498 million is nearly 100 times the redemption outflow, implying strong net new investment activity. The presence of 2,467 active SIPs and 100 active schemes demonstrates a diversified product offering and robust client participation in systematic investing.

\n\n

Investment Analysis

\n

Total investment of ₹498,446,012 represents a net addition to the portfolio, while redemptions of ₹5,000,000 are relatively modest compared to the overall AUM, indicating stable client retention. The investment-to-redemption ratio of approximately 99.7 shows that for every rupee redeemed, roughly 100 rupees are newly invested, supporting portfolio growth.

\n\n

Revenue Analysis

\n

Revenue of ₹2,946,065 translates to an average revenue per client of approximately ₹2,958. Given the high AUM, this revenue level suggests that the current revenue model may be constrained, potentially due to fee structures or the mix of products offered.
